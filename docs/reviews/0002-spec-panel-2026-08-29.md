## Decisions for the author (recommendation first)

  `Deluxe|Expanded|Remaster(ed)?|Edition|Bonus|Anniversary` inside the
  parenthetical — not any parenthetical — so "(Live)" rows stay checkable.

  determiner (idiom). The one regression is the wrong-album form with a
  determiner ("closing cut off the Memories single"); accept it, or add
  `single` to the object-guard list.
